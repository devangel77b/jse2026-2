WIT Internship: Gyro-Arcade Suite Mobile Gamification Platform
Ever since the stone ages, we have found many ways to entertain ourselves through activities and games such as rock paper scissors all the way games that immerse you into its own world through action and visual storytelling. However, a common denominator in every game is its purpose to entertain. WIT is one of the worlds best Gamification platforms. A Gamification platform is a company that solely works on making boring things seem interesting through striking visuals and interactive elements a consumer could use. During our internship, we wanted to take their games to the next level with the help of a gyroscope accessible through mobile devices. By reinforcing our knowledge on CSS, HTML, JavaScript, and learning about React and Github, we built (2-3) minigames that revolve around gyroscopic mechanics with professional grade level visuals, closely mirroring that of a real WIT developer team.
