 Monmouth County Engineering Internship 

Monmonmouth County Engineering is responsible for maintaining, designing, and constructing the county's public infrastructure, including roads, bridges and other transportation facilities. In order to carry out a project start to finish, contractors submit proposals to the county outlining their qualifications for completing a current project. Then a county project manager evaluates the proposals, determining which company is the most suited for the project based on, price, experience and ___. 

During my internship, I worked under a county project manager, and gained hands-on experience observing the process of hiring a contractor to construct a bridge in Manalapan, MN-45. This experience has provided me with a better understanding of civil engineering and the roles various engineers have in aiding our community.
