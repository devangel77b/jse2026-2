\begin{abstract}
During my time at Watermen LLC’s internship, I have been working on various coastal engineering projects. Through them, I have gained experience in fields such as waterfront development projects and permitting. Some of my responsibilities include designing permits for various projects in Autodesk Civil 3D 2024. There, I created drawings that included sounding and tideline claim data. So far, I have contributed to their Lucciola plans for permitting a new dock. This internship has, and will continue to, strengthen my technical drawing, project management, and overall data analysis skills involving coastal engineering practices. 
\end{abstract}
