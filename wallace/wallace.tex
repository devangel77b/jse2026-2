\begin{abstract}
For patients with schizophrenia, maintaining consistent medication adherence can be challenging due to an unwillingness or inability to take medication regularly. Inconsistent treatment can cause a regression in the patient’s progress, potentially requiring increased medical intervention. Extended-release drug delivery systems may be a strategy to reduce the frequency of medication administration, potentially minimizing patient resistance to treatment and improving medication adherence. ACON Pharmaceuticals is developing an extended-release drug delivery system using microspheres —biodegradable polymer particles that encapsulate an active medication for controlled drug release— to deliver cariprazine, an antipsychotic medication used to treat schizophrenia. In this stage, the focus is on developing a method that utilizes an alternative solvent for producing microspheres to improve the efficiency, scalability, and safety of the manufacturing process. The alternative solvent will be evaluated based on its ability to maximize drug loading, minimize waste, and reduce potential for toxic solvent contamination outside of the workspace.
\end{abstract}
