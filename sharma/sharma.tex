Title: Developing Reliable and Context-Aware AI Agents at Commvault
Authors: Ishaan Sharma, Sashank Yellapragada
Abstract draft:
	Artificial intelligence (AI) and large language models (LLMs) are being integrated into software development workflows at a rapidly increasing rate, where AI agents can assist with tasks ranging from research and planning to modifying codebases and reviewing proposed code changes. As these systems take on more complex development tasks, their reliability and ability to efficiently access relevant technical information become increasingly important. Through our internship with Commvault’s AI & Core Systems team, we are investigating these challenges in CVArc, an internal application for agentic software development and task management. Our work focuses on two primary tasks: first, we are examining CVArc’s development workflow and designing automated, deterministic quality-assurance processes that can test different task scenarios in sandbox environments and identify potential failures before the workflow progresses and before changes are implemented; second, we are exploring automated generation of structured knowledge packs that provide AI agents with reusable context about Commvault features and code repositories, reducing the need to repeatedly search large codebases for information. Through these projects, we will develop repeatable methods for testing agentic software systems and providing them with reliable technical context while gaining experience with the architecture and engineering challenges in building production AI development tools.
