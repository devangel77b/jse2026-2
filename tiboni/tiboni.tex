Design and construction of an Arm Mounted Electromagnetic Coilgun

Joseph Tiboni, Paul Zlotnikov

Inspired by tech from the game Cyberpunk 2077, this project aims to explore electromagnetic propulsion by building an arm mounted coilgun. Powered by a capacitor bank and controller with pulse timing circuits, the system accelerates small steel balls through an acrylic tube inside of a thermoformed chassis. The goal is to delve into electromagnetic propulsion that can be used for cost effective and efficient configuration for high speed payload delivery systems. We will explore how coils create electromagnetic fields and how energy can be transferred from copper wire to a steel ball. We plan to look into how different materials and voltages can affect penetration. We want to find the most optimal solution that has the best cost to penetration ratio (C/P).
